# The carousel's story

At the start of this track, the Personalized strategy was a bug waiting to happen and nothing in ToggleWear could have told you when it did. Since then:

- **02** — you instrumented both halves of the app with LaunchDarkly's observability plugins, shipped Personalized the naive way, broke it with one click on the Rollout Tote, and watched the `KeyError` arrive in **Errors**.
- **03** — the flag's **Monitoring** tab showed every error under **Personalized** and none under **Bestsellers**, because the flag evaluation and the exception shared a trace. Then you replayed your own session and watched the carousel vanish.
- **04** — you found the **autogenerated metrics** LaunchDarkly built from your traces, read **User HTTP error rate**'s definition (*lower is better*, per user), and reverted the release.
- **06** — you re-released Personalized behind a **guarded release** watching that real error metric. The regression was caught and rolled back without a human in the loop.
- **07** — you armed an **adaptive trigger**, released Personalized at 100% anyway, and watched LaunchDarkly flip the flag back when the error rate crossed your threshold.

# The idea to keep

Every release has a *before*, a *during*, and an *after*, and each one gets a different safety net:

| Timescale | Mechanism | What it needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Before** | Targeting and segments decide who sees it | A flag |
| **During** | A guarded rollout watches a metric while ramping and rolls back | A flag and an honest metric |
| **After** | An adaptive trigger watches the metric at 100% and switches the variation | The same flag and metric |
| **Any time** | The kill switch | A flag and a human |

Instrumentation is what turns the last column from "a human" into "a metric." Add the plugins once, and every flag you own can be released guarded and left self-healing, with no per-feature code.
